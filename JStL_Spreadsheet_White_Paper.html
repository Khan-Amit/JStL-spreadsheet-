📄 JStL Spreadsheet™ White Paper

Version 1.0 | May 2026 | Author: Amit Khanna

1. Executive Summary

JStL Spreadsheet™ is an installable, offline-capable spreadsheet application that operates independently of Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or any third-party software. It uses proprietary calculation logic, saves data as formatted text files (`.jstl`), and gives users complete ownership of their information.

🎯 Mission: Provide a lightweight, private, portable spreadsheet system that works anywhere — no cloud dependency, no licensing fees, no data harvesting.

2. Problem Statement

Existing spreadsheet solutions have critical flaws:

  • Microsoft Excel: Expensive licenses, proprietary format, vendor lock-in
  • Google Sheets: Requires internet, data stored on third-party servers, privacy concerns
  • Open source alternatives: Complex installation, steep learning curve, inconsistent quality
  • Lock-in formats: Images, PDFs, and proprietary binaries cannot be edited or reused

3. The JStL Solution

JStL Spreadsheet™ solves these problems with:

  • Zero dependencies — runs in any browser, no installation required
  • Offline-first — works without internet after first visit
  • Text-based format — `.jstl` files are plain text with grid formatting, human-readable and machine-readable
  • Installable as PWA — behaves like a native app on any device
  • Perpetual license — pay once, own forever
